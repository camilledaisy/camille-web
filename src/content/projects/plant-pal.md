## Why

Every plant-tracking app I tried wanted me to *log*, *streak*, and *level up*. I just wanted a sticky note that remembers.

## What it does

- one screen, one list of plants
- tap a plant when you water it
- plants you've forgotten slowly turn a little bit yellow
