## The question

Everyone says nobody reads anymore. Twelve participants kept a two-week diary of everything longer than a tweet they read on their phone.

## A few findings (placeholder)

1. People read the most in lines — for coffee, for trains, for doctors.
2. "Saving for later" is where articles go to die.
3. Almost everyone had one app they felt guilty about.
