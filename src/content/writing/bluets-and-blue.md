Maggie Nelson's *Bluets* is 240 numbered propositions about the color blue, heartbreak, and looking closely.

After I finished it, I started a list. Blue things, one a day. The recycling bin behind the laundromat. The hour right after sunset. A particular ballpoint pen.

It turns out that when you promise to look for something, it starts looking back.
