I reread *The Days of Abandonment* this month, and the strangest part wasn't the book. It was the pencil marks.

Six years ago, I underlined sentences about anger. This time, I kept stopping at sentences about kitchens — the light in them, the dishes, the way a room keeps going when a person stops.

> A book doesn't change between readings. It's the only thing in the room that doesn't.

## The margins are a diary

I've started to think of my old annotations as a very unreliable diary. They don't tell me what happened to me that year, but they tell me what I was *listening for*.

Some things I apparently cared about in 2020:

- exclamation points (three, in one margin)
- the word "yes" written very small
- a grocery list, for reasons unknown

## What I want to try

Reading the same book once a year, on purpose, like a ritual. Using a different color pencil each time. Seeing the layers build up like sediment.
