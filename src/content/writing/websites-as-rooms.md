When I was twelve, I had a website with a guestbook, a hit counter, and a page that was just a list of songs I liked. Nobody asked for it. It was mine.

Old personal websites were *places*. You arrived. You wandered. You noticed the wallpaper. Someone had decided where the furniture went.

## Feeds don't have furniture

A feed is a hallway that never ends. Nothing stays where you left it.

What I want from the internet now is smaller: a few rooms, lived in, a little messy. A place where the person who made it left fingerprints on everything.
